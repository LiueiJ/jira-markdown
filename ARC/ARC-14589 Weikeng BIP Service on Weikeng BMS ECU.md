# ARC-14589 Weikeng: BIP Service on Weikeng BMS ECU

> [!jira] New ·  · [[Nguyen_Quoc_Su_(MSETA-Hub-CN)|Nguyen Quoc Su (MS/ETA-Hub-CN)]] · 更新于 2026-06-16T13:55:31.000+0000

## 描述

The RTA BIP enables you customer demands to quickly build-up experience using RTA solutions, prior to starting developments for series projects.

As part of this position, ETAS will deliver ETAS’ AUTOSAR Starter kit using version RTA-CAR 12.9.0 ported to S32K358 on Evaluation Board, using the GHS2022.1.4 compiler. This delivery will contain an integrated RTA-OS, RTA-RTE, RTA-BSW and MCAL from NXP. In addition, ETAS will also conduct a 5 days support at Weikeng to familiarize the team on the ETAS tool chain as well as the Starter kit.
