## 描述

We are currently tracking a project where Jinmai is securing a Geely integrated front-view unit project from a Tier 1 supplier. We are still gathering detailed project information. In the meantime, please assist in analyzing the enterprise standards provided by the customer, as well as the engineering services ETAS can offer.

1. Project Status & Commercial Progress
  • A certain Tier 1 supplier has already been awarded the Geely project. Jinmai is currently negotiating a collaboration opportunity with this Tier 1. They are presently discussing commercial terms, and it is highly likely that Jinmai will be selected.
  • Jinmai wants to understand ETAS’s project experience regarding Geely’s BaseTech enterprise standards, including how to divide the work and collaborate.
  • Software Quotation Requirements: Jinmai currently requires a quotation for RTA-CAR + CycurHSM, along with engineering services to adapt to the BaseTech 3.0 enterprise standards. ECM SF needs to discuss with the customer to define the scope and provide a quotation for the engineering services.
2. Key Milestones
  • October 2026: Deliver the first version of samples (the time left for our adaptation and debugging is very limited).
  • April 2027: Project SOP (Start of Production).
3. Technical Configuration & Potential Risks
  • Chip & Toolchain: IFX TC377, using Tasking compiler (specific version to be confirmed), software based on Base Tech 3.0 / 3.5.
  • Information Security (Major Risk): Jinmai plans to use the CycurHSM solution; whether this meets the requirements of BaseTech 3.0/3.5 remains to be confirmed.
4. Next Steps / Action Items
  • Documentation: The customer will send over the requirement documents and test specifications, which require an in-depth evaluation from ECM.
  ◦ Base Tech Software Requirement-ADCU_ASDM-V07(BTV08)_20250929.pdf
  ◦ GEEA3.0-24R3_CSRS-ADCU&ASDM.pdf
  • Quotation: Please provide a quotation proposal for RTA-CAR, CycurHSM, and the engineering services for adapting to BaseTech 3.0 enterprise standards.
  • Regarding the FBL adapted to Geely’s enterprise standards: If there is a mature solution at a reasonable price, the customer will consider purchasing it.
  • Technical Pre-research: Regarding the engineering services for BaseTech 3.0 adaptation, please organize an internal technical sync meeting first to evaluate potential risks. Please provide your feedback by this Friday. The internal meeting is scheduled for next Monday.
