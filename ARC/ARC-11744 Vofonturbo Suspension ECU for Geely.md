## 描述

**Background Infor:** Vofonturbo is a subsidiary of Geely group, the main product is turbocharger and suspension ECU. They are sourcing CP supplier for the suspension ECU development.

**Competition Status:**

- **Vector:** share the Geely group’s  lowest discount frame contract, total **2.2M CNY** for “development license+ production license+ FBL”
- **Hirain:** offer very low price
- **ETAS**

**Sourcing strategy:**

- To assure the project timing and successful SOP, only consider global suppliers.
- Due to strong relationship with customer, Vofonturbo will try to convince their management to  consider single-sourcing to ETAS if we can meet the target price.
- Target price: 2.55M CNY

**Project Info:**

- ECU: Suspension ECU
- Chip: S32K312
- Compiler: GCC
- SOP: M.2026
