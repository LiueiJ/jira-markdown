> [!note]+ 2026-07-06 03:17 · [[JI_Jiaqi_(ETAS-ECMXSF-CN)|JI Jiaqi (ETAS-ECM/XSF-CN)]]
> **BIP Process Optimization Objective:**
> To improve delivery efficiency, shorten the project delivery cycle, and simplify the overall BIP process, it is proposed to streamline the current workflow by removing the **Project Start (PS)** phase. Under the optimized process, all delivery artifacts will undergo only a single **Quality Goal (QG)** review, which will be conducted at the final **QG2** milestone. This approach reduces redundant review activities, improves project execution efficiency, and ensures that the required quality standards are still achieved before delivery.
