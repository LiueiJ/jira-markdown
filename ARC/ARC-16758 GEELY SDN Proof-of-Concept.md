**Geely SDN Current status :**

- Geely E/E architecture team considers SDN as a high priority topic.

- There is a Geely Bi-Weekly internal meeting providing progress updates to the Executive Director of the E/E arch. & Software team. ( This is very high-level management in Geely )
- ETAS China ( Lihua & KAM ) has met Geely Executive Director - 3 times in the past reg. the same topic.
- Since the last 2 weeks, there has been no updates from ETAS ( Local & Global ) on this topic . Sales fear that ETAS may lose trust & confidence with Geely if there is no progress or the progress is very slow from our end.
- ETCN Sales are planning for a meeting with Geely for this Friday – 3rd July and expect ETAS Global experts support to engage with Geely to discuss about SDN topic in detail.

**Topics of interest :**

 

- Geely is mainly interested in the FOTA use case and want to know about other use case scenarios our experts have to offer for SDN.  For instance – DevOps.
- Geely expects us to elaborate our whitepaper on SDN topic.

In contrast, Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN) is mentioned as a lower priority for Geely. Since they have already in production with TSN , they are less interested in that topic.
