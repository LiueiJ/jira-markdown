# ARC-9493 ETCN - Customers & CN Market Requirements for RTA-CAR Product

> [!jira] New ·  · [[Buttle_Darren_(ETAS-ECMPRM1-EMW)|Buttle Darren (ETAS-ECM/PRM1-EMW)]] · 更新于 2025-10-09T05:08:29.000+0000
> [在 Jira 中打开](https://jira.etas-dev.com/browse/ARC-9493)

## 描述

RTA-CAR workshop conducted in China during 6th-7th of March'2025. The goal of the workshop was: 

***"Better define Chinese OEM and Tier 1 user requirements so that we, RTA-CAR, can*** ***better meet their needs and improve our market fit in China"***

**Workshop highlights :**

- 6 Interviews were conducted ahead of the workshop to identify 4 top level focus areas:
  - Multicore
  - Artifact Importing
  - General Automation
  - ConfGen Automation
- During the workshop we reflected on the  user's journey through each of these topic areas and documented the current pain points.
- Completed a number of voting and prioritization activities to ensure a clear break down of 'Must Have' vs 'Nice To Have' Needs.
- Captured the customer requirements & pain points through ~20+ engineers working with customers
- All feedback was captured and refined down into the clear user requirements - these were converted into Need tickets linked to this motivation.
- Any topics that did not fit into these 4 areas, were captured as "Parking Lot" topics. Further interviews were completed after the workshop to define the associated Needs Tickets.

For information on the running of this workshop and the definition of the outcomes - contact [Parmar Kishan (ETAS-ECM/XPC-Yok1) [X]](https://jira.etas-dev.com/secure/ViewProfile.jspa?name=prk1yok)
