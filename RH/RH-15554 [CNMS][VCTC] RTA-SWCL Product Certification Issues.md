> as per L3-issue LIB-121:
>
> We have a NIST CAVP evaluated CycurLIB version (CAVP is validating the implementation of the algorithms), which is one part of FIPS 140-3. 
>
> Since CycurLIB or RTA-SWCL (which is a subset of CycurLIB) is raw C code, it can't be FIPS 140-3 certified, this can only be done in combination with HW, as a final binary.
>
> Same applies for IEC1970.
>
> And also CC EAL is evaluating ta target of evaluation, which is bound to a specific HW.
>
>
>
> We are working to get a certificate for chinese standard in future, but this isn't available yet.
