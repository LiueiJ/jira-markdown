## 描述

GAC is conducting an SDN pre-study for its next-generation **5.0 E/E architecture** and is looking for an SDN solution centered around its **SVCC** platform. ( SVCC networking, routing, switch configuration, and Realtek switch support.)

### **Customer Requirements (High-Level)**

GAC expects the SDN solution to support:

- CAN-to-CAN routing
- Ethernet-to-Ethernet routing
- Hardware-based routing
- Ethernet switch configuration
- Phase 1: Static network configuration
- Phase 2: Dynamic network configuration
- Strong interest in ETAS capabilities and development experience with **Realtek switches**.

### **Requested Support from ETAS**

- Join an online technical workshop with GAC.
- Present the ETAS SDN architecture and demo.
- Discuss potential solutions for SVCC, routing, and switch configuration.
- Identify technical gaps and define possible PoC next steps.
