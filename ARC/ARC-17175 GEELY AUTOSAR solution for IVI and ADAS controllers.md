# ARC-17175 GEELY AUTOSAR solution for IVI and ADAS controllers

## 描述

Geely is currently evaluating a CP+HSM solution for its IVI and ADAS controller programs and is assessing whether the proposed ETAS platform can meet its future Ethernet networking and TSN (Time-Sensitive Networking) requirements. As the opportunity progresses, the customer requires clear technical evidence and roadmap visibility to determine the feasibility of adopting the solution.

This information is needed to support ongoing technical discussions with Geely, address feasibility questions, and provide confidence in the proposed solution's capability and future development direction. A timely response is required due to upcoming customer alignments and decision-making activities.
